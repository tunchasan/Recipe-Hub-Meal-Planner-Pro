Questions, answered

Is Recipe Hub free?

Yes. You can download Recipe Hub for free, browse recipes and create your first AI meal plan. Pro unlocks unlimited plans, exclusive recipes, grocery lists, nutrition info and the water challenge.

Which diets does it support?

Flexible, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, low carb, gluten free and halal. You can also tell the AI Coach which foods to avoid, such as nuts, dairy or eggs.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your App Store account. Open your account's Subscriptions settings and choose Recipe Hub. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged.

Can I save recipes from other websites?

Yes. Paste a link from any recipe site and Recipe Hub saves it alongside your plans and favorites.
